## Part 2 — Verdict (required)

Tiers, omit empty: feel-breaking → missed simplifications → performance → interruptibility → origin/cohesion → a11y.

- **Block** — feel-breaking, keyboard/100+/day motion, `scale(0)`/`ease-in` on UI, or non-GPU with an easy GPU fix.
