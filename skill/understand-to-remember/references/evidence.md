# Evidence behind each rule

Each source has one note in `sources/` with its citation, a link to the original, the design, the findings, and what the note was written from.

The idea is Daniel Willingham's: memories are the residue of thought ([Willingham 2008](sources/willingham-2008.md)), a theme of his book ([Willingham 2009](sources/willingham-2009.md)).

## Core claim

- Meaning versus form at study time: [Craik & Tulving 1975](sources/craik-tulving-1975.md), [Hyde & Jenkins 1973](sources/hyde-jenkins-1973.md), [Willingham 2003](sources/willingham-2003.md)
- Forgetting after deep encoding: [Peng et al. 2024](sources/peng-2024.md), [Lawrence et al. 2024](sources/lawrence-2024.md)
- Ranking of study techniques: [Dunlosky et al. 2013](sources/dunlosky-2013.md), [Donoghue & Hattie 2021](sources/donoghue-hattie-2021.md)

## Step 1: starting point

- Prior knowledge and recall: [Recht & Leslie 1988](sources/recht-leslie-1988.md)
- Matching practice to the test: [Morris, Bransford & Franks 1977](sources/morris-bransford-franks-1977.md)

## Step 2: frame first

- Context before versus after the material: [Bransford & Johnson 1972](sources/bransford-johnson-1972.md)

## Step 3: explain each piece

- How experts and novices sort physics problems: [Chi, Feltovich & Glaser 1981](sources/chi-feltovich-glaser-1981.md)
- Guided versus unassisted discovery: [Alfieri et al. 2011](sources/alfieri-2011.md)
- Guidance for experienced learners: [Kalyuga et al. 2003](sources/kalyuga-2003.md)

## Step 4: the learner does the thinking

## Step 5: new case

## Pitfalls

- Interesting but irrelevant details: [Sundararajan & Adesope 2020](sources/sundararajan-adesope-2020.md)
- Curiosity and memory: [Gruber et al. 2014](sources/gruber-2014.md)
- Decay of knowledge that stops being used: [Bahrick & Hall 1991](sources/bahrick-hall-1991.md), [Pawl et al. 2012](sources/pawl-2012.md)

## Limits

Most classic experiments use word lists or short texts and measure memory over minutes to weeks. In long-term studies, understanding, later use and review are confounded. No study compares a formula mnemonic with explaining what the formula means.
