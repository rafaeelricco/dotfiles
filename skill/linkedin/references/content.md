# Content

Posting is the fastest way to move SSI pillar 3, and the one most people skip. A profile
with no activity has no reach, however well it is written.

Every post must be legible as coming from the person the profile describes. Content
disconnected from the stated positioning trains the algorithm on the wrong audience.
Missing company/result/topic → `[brackets]` in the post draft; no pre-post questionnaire.

## Five post types

**1 Technical** — explain one thing from the field simply.

> Three mistakes in consultative B2B selling that quietly cut conversion.

**2 Behind the scenes** — how the work actually gets organized.

> How I structure feedback so expectations stop being a guessing game.

**3 Practical learning** — something applied, including something that went wrong.

> What a deploy that did not go to plan taught me about rollback discipline.

**4 Strategic opinion** — a market shift, with a position on it.

> Why data beats instinct in marketing decisions, and where it does not.

**5 Result, without bragging** — a delivery, framed as work rather than a trophy.

> Closing the quarter at 15% higher operational efficiency and 30% fewer delays.

## Never post

Complaints about an employer, emotional venting, internal conflict, criticism of colleagues
or leadership, indirect jabs, polarizing politics, anything disconnected from the stated
positioning.

The reason is specific: a recruiter reading a public complaint about a former employer
prices in the same treatment of their own company. It is a hiring risk, and it is silent —
nobody tells the candidate that is why.

## Comments

2-4 lines with substance: a technical opinion, practical experience that extends the post,
or a real question. Comments on posts from established people in the field put the profile
in front of their audience, which is the cheapest reach available.

`Congrats`, a bare emoji, and generic agreement do nothing. Arguments do worse.

## Rhythm

- 5-10 relevant interactions per day — comments, reactions, shares with a line of your own.
- 3 authored posts per week, on fixed days.
- Roughly 30 minutes per day.

Fixed days matter more than the specific days. Choose two or three and hold them.

## Checklist

Topics match the positioning in the headline and About. Post type named for each draft.
Fixed publishing days chosen. Comment drafts carry an opinion, experience, or question, and
run 2-4 lines. Nothing in the never-post categories.
