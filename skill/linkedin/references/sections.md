# Sections below the fold

Heterogeneous by topic, uniform by contract: every item here is advisory and
checklist-shaped. Ordered by what a recruiter can filter on, because that is what decides
whether the profile is ever returned. Do not re-split this file by course module.

## Skills — recruiters filter on this field

Keep adding until LinkedIn stops you. The cap has moved before and will move again; the
instruction is "fill it", not a number.

Technical before behavioral. `Proactive` and `team player` are not search terms — nobody
queries them, and they displace terms that are queried.

Take the whole ranked list from `positioning`, not a subset.

## Recommendations

Target 5 or more. Ask ex-managers, current and former peers, stakeholders, and clients —
anyone who saw the work.

Send one before asking for one; the reply rate is not close. Be specific in the request
about which project and which strength you want mentioned, or you get a paragraph of
adjectives.

## Education and courses

Formal degrees only in education: graduation, postgraduate, MBA, master's, doctorate. Short
courses go under courses.

Course format: `Name - Institution (year)`. Strategic and recent only — a wall of
introductory certificates dilutes the ones that matter.

## Profile URL

First and last name, shortest form still available. Add a number or hyphen only when the
plain form is taken.

## Open to Work

Do **not** turn on the photo badge. Use the recruiters-only setting instead:

`Jobs → Preferences → Open to work → visibility: Recruiters only`

Colleagues at the current employer do not see it, which is what makes it usable while
employed. If employment status is known, do not re-ask; if unknown, bracket and ask at the
end. Career transition: preferred titles = **target** role from positioning, not legacy
titles.

> Course claim, unverified: the public badge correlates with fewer recruiter contacts. The
> recruiters-only setting is the better default regardless, because it works while employed.

## Premium

Not required. It does not improve ranking or placement in recruiter search. The messaging
allowance is the only real benefit. A free trial is the only version worth taking.

## Checklist

Skills field filled to the cap, technical first. 5+ recommendations requested, one sent
first. Degrees under education, short courses under courses in
`Name - Institution (year)` form. URL shortened to a name. Open to Work recruiters-only,
badge off; transition titles = target role.
