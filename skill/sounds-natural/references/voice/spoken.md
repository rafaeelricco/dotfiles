# Spoken examples

Raw lines from real calls, then the version that follows profile.md. Rules: profile.md.
A Raw line is an excerpt; its Polished version may use facts stated elsewhere in the same call. When
rewriting, use only facts the user gave you.
Anything private (pay, contracts, dates, who was in the room) stays out of this file.

## Intro, stack

Raw: "mainly working with python typescript react … in the last in the for in the last year i have i have been
working on most on distributed event sourced agent-based systems"
Polished: "My main stack is Python, TypeScript, and React, and for the last year I've been building
agent-based systems on a distributed, event-sourced backend."
Rule: one idea per sentence.

## Ending

Raw: "creating AI products, automation and things like that."
Polished: "Now I'm looking for a role where I can keep building AI products and automations, from the idea to production."
Rule: end on the result, then stop.

## Specific beats vague

Raw: "Voice mode was very difficult, but I sorted it and the result was good."
Polished: "Sometimes it didn't detect the person, sometimes it heard noise in the room, and sometimes the
connection dropped. I fixed those problems one by one, and when the connection fails, it falls back to text."
Rule: lead with a problem or result.

## Side project

Raw: "uh i i have my own uh hermes agent running in and you know find a new job is is a really annoying process"
Polished: "That sounds very close to something I built for myself. Job hunting creates a lot of email, so I set
up my own agents to read it. Every morning they report what changed."
Rule: one idea per sentence.

## Opening claim

Raw: "I'm an expert in AI workflows and agent-based"
Polished: "I build agent-based systems, and for the last year that's been most of my work."
Rule: no self-ratings; say what he does, not how good he is.

## Explaining expertise

Raw: "which means I have deeply understanding about the Igentic Harness and how AI works."
Polished: "In practice that means the loop that decides when the model calls a tool, and what it is allowed to do."
Rule: replace the adjective with the mechanism.

## Project pitch

Raw: "And basically the clients wanted a software for UK state agencies where a visitor was"
Polished: "The clients were UK estate agencies. They wanted visitors on their own website to talk to an assistant
and arrange a viewing, by text or by voice."
Rule: one idea per sentence; no "basically".

## Impact without a number

Raw: "giving profits for the clients. So,"
Polished: "It's live with real customers. I don't have a revenue number I can defend, so I'd rather not guess one."
Rule: never claim an outcome that can't be backed up.

## Scale claim

Raw: "hundreds of users."
Polished: "I don't have the current user count in front of me. What I can say is that it's in production."
Rule: no number he can't source.

## Ownership

Raw: "Also, I built the front-end" / "and the backend of it. Yes, that was a lot."
Polished: "I owned the client-facing side end to end, and the agent runtime behind it. The rest of the backend was
the team's."
Rule: "I" only for his own scope.

## Rating his own work

Raw: "It's a pretty beautiful project, yeah."
Polished: "The part I like most is that onboarding is three steps: a URL, a calendar connection, and one script tag."
Rule: give the detail and let them judge.

## A question he can't answer

Raw: "Yeah, this question that is putting me to think so."
Polished: "I don't have a strong example of that. The closest is …" Or, when one exists, start with it.
Rule: no praise, no stalling; answer first, or name the honest limit.

## Undercutting himself

Raw: "I'm a bit scared with technical chats not because I'm I'm dumb or something like"
Polished: "I prepare a lot for technical rounds, because I like going into detail rather than staying on the surface."
Rule: state the habit, not the fear.

## Why this company

Raw: "To be honest, I didn't take a look in the website and the whole purpose. I have been so busy in the last days"
Polished: "What caught my attention is the agent platform itself: running agents safely, with the right
permissions. That's the layer I want to work on."
Rule: answer with what he wants, not with what he didn't do.

## Weak ending

Raw: "Yeah, it's just that. I don't know if I answered your question, but it's..."
Polished: "So that's why I'd pick the simpler option here." Then stop, and let them ask.
Rule: end on the point; don't audit yourself out loud.
